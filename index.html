How It Works

Your quick start guide to managing your anime watchlist

1

Adding a Series & Auto-Filling

Click the "Add Anime" button. Type the title and click "Auto-Fill" to instantly fetch episode counts and genres from MyAnimeList!

2

Tracking Seasons, Arcs & Movies

Click the chevron arrow ( > ) next to any anime row to expand its details. From there, click "Add Entry" to track individual seasons or movie arcs.

3

Searching & Filtering

Use the search bar to find titles instantly, or click the status filter buttons (like Watching or Completed) to organize your view.

4

Cloud Sync & Security

Your data is securely stored in Supabase and tied to your personal login account. Access your watchlist from any device anytime.
